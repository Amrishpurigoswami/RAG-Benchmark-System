The board meeting of TechCorp held on June 20, 2025, chaired by CEO Anjali Verma, recorded decisions on five agenda items: approval of the Q1 FY2025 financial review, status update of Project Alpha (on track for a September launch), deferral of the employee bonus policy revision, approval of a new office lease in Mumbai BKC, and approval of a diversity hiring initiative with a 25 lakh budget.
